# Uniform remainder: a bounded feasibility proof

September 16, 2026. This note proves the stability and uniform remainder target
in [the proposed claim](predictive-claim.md), with explicit constants. It is a
local proof with independent arithmetic checks, pending independent proof review
and human mathematical review. It does not establish novelty, a general ordering
after tuning, or a guarantee for estimated preprocessing. The conference
manuscript and frozen studies retain their existing claims.

## Statement

Use the model, exact moment map $L$, triangular surrogate $L_0$, first feedback
correction, and finite nonnegative remainder $E_{W,k}$ in (P1)–(P5) of the
[supporting note](predictive-claim.md). In particular, centering and scales are
known, the intercept is fixed, initialization is zero, and each SGD update uses
$B$ fresh independent observations. Keep the original coefficient scale and the
factor $1/2$ in both risks.

Write $b=q(1-q)$ and let

$$
  0<q\leq1/16,\quad |\ell|\leq1,\quad B\in\mathbb N,\quad
  0<u\leq1/4,\quad 4bu\leq v\leq u,\quad
  \theta=\frac{v}{bB}\leq1,\qquad \zeta=\frac{u}{B}+\theta.
$$

This includes precisely the drift ratios generated by the proposal's
$0\leq\delta\leq1/2$. It does not include variance scaling. Define

$$
  \lambda=1-v/4,\qquad h=(1,1,2)^\top,\qquad
  \mu_W=|w_W|^\top h,
$$

where absolute values of vectors and matrices are entrywise. Then

$$
  \mu_C=\frac18+\frac{q|\ell|}{2}+b,\qquad
  \mu_{C_R}=\frac18+\frac{|\ell|(1-q)}2+(1-q)^2.
$$

**Proposition.** Throughout this domain, $\rho(|L|)<\lambda<1$, so the exact SGD
recurrence is mean-square stable. For every $k\geq0$, signal, and
$\sigma^2\geq0$, the finite remainder already defined in (P5) satisfies

$$
  |R_{W,k}-\widehat R_{W,k}|\leq E_{W,k}
  \leq \frac{8\mu_Wq^2}{1-4q}
  \left[\|\beta^*\|_2^2\lambda^k+2\sigma^2\zeta\right]. \tag{F1}
$$

Since $\lambda^k\leq e^{-kv/4}$, $\mu_C\leq7/32$, $\mu_{C_R}\leq13/8$, and
$1-4q\geq3/4$, this proves the original uniform envelope (P2) with **$K=104/3$**
for both objectives. More informative separate constants are

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   E_{C,k}&\leq \frac73q^2
    \left[\|\beta^*\|_2^2e^{-kv/4}+2\sigma^2\zeta\right],\\
   E_{C_R,k}&\leq \frac{52}{3}q^2
    \left[\|\beta^*\|_2^2e^{-kv/4}+2\sigma^2\zeta\right].
  \end{aligned} \tag{F2}
$$

The constants are sufficient, not optimal. The parameter-specific factor in (F1)
is preferable to (F2), and direct finite propagation (P5) can be much sharper.
The zero-step predictor is handled separately, with zero approximation error as
in the proposal.

## Positive comparison matrices

Set $Q=|L_0|$, $J=|L-L_0|$, and $A=|L|$. The only overlapping nonzero entries of
$L_0$ and $L-L_0$ are positive middle diagonal entries. Thus $A=Q+J$ exactly.
Introduce

$$
  \kappa=\frac{|c|}{b}=\frac{|\ell|}{2(1-q)},\qquad
  \chi=1-v-\theta(1-4b),\qquad
  \omega=v\kappa^2(1-1/B)+\theta/4.
$$

The domain gives

$$
  \kappa\leq\frac8{15},\qquad -v\leq\chi\leq1-v,\qquad
  \omega\leq\frac{289}{900},\qquad \theta\geq\frac{4u}{B}. \tag{F3}
$$

The last inequality follows from $v\geq4bu$. The exact triangular map is

$$
  Q=\begin{pmatrix}
   1-u(2-u)&0&0\\
   v(1-u)\kappa&1-u-v(1-u)&0\\
   v\omega&2v\kappa|\chi|&1-v(1+\chi)
  \end{pmatrix}. \tag{F4}
$$

Its diagonal entries are nonnegative by their original expressions in (P3). The
quantity $\chi$ can be negative on the domain; the absolute values in (F4) are
essential.

For the feedback matrix, put

$$
  a_H=\theta(1-q)+\frac{v\ell^2(1-1/B)}{1-q}.
$$

Its nonzero entries are

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   J_{12}&=4qu(1-u)|\ell|,\\
   J_{13}&=4qu^2\{q\ell^2(1-1/B)+(1-q)/B\}\leq4qu^2,\\
   J_{22}&=qu a_H,\qquad J_{23}=2qu|\ell|\,|\chi|.
  \end{aligned} \tag{F5}
$$

Indices in this note start at one. We will use the bound

$$
  a_H+4|\chi|\leq4. \tag{F6}
$$

To prove it when $\chi\geq0$, substitute its definition and bound
$a_H\leq\theta(1-q)+v/(1-q)$ to obtain

$$
  a_H+4\chi\leq4
    -v\left(4-\frac1{1-q}\right)
    -\theta\{4(1-4b)-(1-q)\}\leq4.
$$

Both subtracted coefficients are positive: the second is $3-15q+16q^2\geq17/8$.
When $\chi<0$, use $|\chi|\leq v\leq1/4$ and $a_H\leq(1-q)+1/\{4(1-q)\}\leq5/4$,
giving $a_H+4|\chi|\leq9/4<4$.

## Three vector inequalities

Let

$$
  p=(1,1/2,1)^\top,\qquad n=(1,1/2,2)^\top,\qquad
  G_\lambda=\lambda I-Q.
$$

The proof needs only the following entrywise inequalities:

$$
  \begin{gathered}
   G_\lambda p>0,\qquad G_\lambda h>0,\\
   Jp\leq2qG_\lambda h,\qquad Jh\leq4qG_\lambda h,\\
   |g|\leq\zeta(I-Q)n.
  \end{gathered} \tag{F7}
$$

Here $g$ is the unit-noise input in (P3).

For the first line, direct substitution in (F4) gives the lower bounds

$$
  G_\lambda p\geq
  \begin{pmatrix}3u/2\\41u/120\\41v/900\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  G_\lambda h\geq
  \begin{pmatrix}3u/2\\u\\371v/900\end{pmatrix}. \tag{F8}
$$

For example, the second coordinate for $p$ is $u/2+v(1-u)(1/2-\kappa)-v/8$,
bounded below using $\kappa-1/2\leq1/30$ and $v\leq u$. For $h$ it is
$u+v\{(1-u)(1-\kappa)-1/4\}\geq u+v/10$. The third coordinates divided by $v$
are

$$
  3/4+\chi-\omega-\kappa|\chi|,\qquad
  3/2+2\chi-\omega-2\kappa|\chi|.
$$

For $\chi\geq0$, drop the nonnegative terms involving $\chi$. For $\chi<0$, use
$|\chi|\leq1/4$ and (F3), obtaining the constants in (F8).

Equations (F5)–(F6) give

$$
  Jp\leq qu(5/2,2,0)^\top,\qquad
  Jh\leq qu(5,4,0)^\top.
$$

These bounds and (F8) prove the second line of (F7). In particular, feedback
enters only the first two coordinates, whose deficits in (F8) are proportional
to the faster drift $u$. This avoids an artificial factor $u/v$ when
accumulating feedback over the rare learning time.

For the noise inequality, (F3) implies $\zeta\geq5u/B$. Since

$$
  |g|=(4u^2/B,\;2qu|\ell|\theta,\;v\theta)^\top,
$$

we have $|g|\leq\zeta(4u/5,u/8,v)^\top$. Also,

$$
  (I-Q)n\geq(7u/4,7u/15,941v/900)^\top. \tag{F9}
$$

The third coordinate divided by $v$ is $2+2\chi-\omega-\kappa|\chi|$. The
negative-$\chi$ case gives $2-(2+8/15)/4-289/900=941/900$; the nonnegative case
has a larger lower bound. This proves the final line of (F7).

## Stability and accumulation of feedback

The nonnegative resolvent

$$
  \mathcal K=(\lambda I-Q)^{-1}J
    =\sum_{j\geq0}\lambda^{-j-1}Q^jJ
$$

exists by (F8). Equations (F7) imply

$$
  \mathcal Kp\leq2qh,\qquad \mathcal Kh\leq4qh,
  \qquad (I-\mathcal K)^{-1}\mathcal Kh\leq\frac{4q}{1-4q}h. \tag{F10}
$$

The last inequality follows by summing nonnegative powers, since $4q\leq1/4<1$.
Furthermore,

$$
  (\lambda I-A)h=G_\lambda h-Jh
  \geq(1-4q)G_\lambda h>0.
$$

The induced maximum norm weighted by $h$ therefore gives
$\rho(L)\leq\rho(A)<\lambda<1$. This establishes stability on the entire domain
without a numerical eigenvalue calculation. We will also use

$$
  (\lambda I-A)^{-1}J=(I-\mathcal K)^{-1}\mathcal K. \tag{F11}
$$

Split the nonnegative recurrence (P5) into signal and unit-noise parts, denoted
by superscripts $F$ and $N$. Write $S=\|\beta^*\|_2^2$ and use $v_k$ for the
vector in (P5), distinct from the scalar drift $v$. The initial vector satisfies
$|s_0|\leq Sp$, by $2|\beta_C^*\beta_R^*|\leq S$. Thus

$$
  v_k^F\leq S\lambda^kp,\qquad
  a_k^F=\sum_{j<k}Q^{k-1-j}Jv_j^F
  \leq S\lambda^k\mathcal Kp\leq2qS\lambda^kh.
$$

Accumulating the second feedback with (F10)–(F11) yields

$$
  r_k^F\leq2qS\lambda^k(\lambda I-A)^{-1}Jh
  \leq\frac{8q^2}{1-4q}S\lambda^kh. \tag{F12}
$$

For unit noise, (F7) and zero initialization give $v_k^N\leq\zeta n\leq\zeta h$
by induction. The nonnegative series for $(I-Q)^{-1}$ and $(I-A)^{-1}$ are
bounded entrywise by their corresponding $\lambda$ resolvents. Consequently,

$$
  a_k^N\leq\zeta(I-Q)^{-1}Jh\leq4q\zeta h,
  \qquad
  r_k^N\leq4q\zeta(I-A)^{-1}Jh
  \leq\frac{16q^2}{1-4q}\zeta h. \tag{F13}
$$

Multiplying (F12) plus $\sigma^2$ times (F13) by $|w_W|^\top$ proves (F1). The
argument has no lower bound on $q$ or $u$, no division by $u-v$, and no
restriction on $k$. It therefore includes coincident learning rates, all three
batch-rarity regimes allowed by the domain, and arbitrarily long runs.

## Verification and usefulness

Inside `devenv shell`, run `make check-predictive-feasibility`. The separate
[checker](../experiments/check_predictive_feasibility.py) constructs exact
rational moment operators by averaging the four possible random update matrices.
It checks (F7), stability margins, and the identity $A=Q+J$ at boundary and
interior configurations, including extremely small prevalence and rates and
cases with negative $\chi$. The 1,125 rational configurations include 80 with
negative $\chi$. This construction does not reuse the closed-form moment
recurrence. A second check compares (F1) with finite (P5) propagation at 7,200
endpoints over both objectives, signal signs, and long training durations. The
largest finite-bound/envelope ratio is 0.199499. These checks support the
algebra; the proof above supplies the uniform claim.

The checker also reports the envelope at the existing C1/C2 anchors. This is a
check of whether the proved constants are informative, using floating-point
evaluation rather than new outward-rounded certificates. It retains comparisons
whose envelope overlaps zero. No new law is called a held-out prediction, and no
new rate search is performed.

parameter-specific envelopes from (F1). The envelope column uses $e^{-kv/4}$ in
place of the slightly sharper $\lambda^k$.

The next scientific step is an explicit predictive regime and a comparison over
