Normalization Influences Shrinkage in Regularized Regression*. Its checked-out
commit at setup was `3838efaeb997156440d85cbe7413f7d75cfd56ed`.

were uncommitted working files in that project. The commit above therefore

The investigation was copied to `notes/two-feature-investigation.md`, with
Markdown math delimiters and local links adapted. The code was adapted to
`experiments/check_two_features.py`, adding command-line control and result
provenance. `results/two-feature-check.json` is regenerated by that adapted
script; it is not a byte-for-byte copy of the original output.

The manuscript synthesizes the mathematical derivations and distinguishes
established bivariate results from the proposed general research program. It
does not claim that the complete follow-up study or novelty review is done. The
original manuscript and package have not been modified by this setup.

The local `tex_article` Cookiecutter template was inspected for conventions. Its
generic preamble and structure were replaced by a smaller manuscript scaffold
suited to the existing mathematics and the devenv build.

On September 14, 2026, the research direction shifted to rare-feature learning
under normalization, early stopping, and SGD. The earlier shrinkage sections and
numerical reference results were retained as baselines. New manuscript sections
document elementary population gradient-flow and scalar fresh-batch SGD
calculations, checked independently in `experiments/check_optimization.py`. The
general finite-time theory and empirical optimizer comparisons remain open.
