\title{Normalization and Shrinkage with Correlated Binary Features}
\author{Johan Larsson}
\date{Working manuscript --- September 14, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This working theoretical investigation studies how correlation changes the
effect of normalization in penalized linear regression with binary features.
For two features, we derive an exact selection integral for the weighted
elastic net and identify normalization conclusions that survive correlation.
Variance weighting preserves the noiseless lasso solution for a single signal,
but shrinkage with two active signals depends on their signs and on covariance
divided by feature variance. A rare binary feature can consequently retain a
nonvanishing effect from another coefficient's shrinkage even as Pearson
correlation tends to zero. We give explicit counterexamples, an approximation
bound, and a formal Gaussian limit, and separate these results from the open
problem of a general sampling theorem. The manuscript establishes a foundation
for further work; literature novelty and empirical usefulness remain to be
assessed.
\end{abstract}
