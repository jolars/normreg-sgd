\section*{AI Use Statement}

Generative AI tools, including OpenAI Codex, were used substantially in this
project for research planning, literature search, mathematical derivation and
proof review, implementation of experiments and independent numerical checks,
interpretation of results, and manuscript drafting and editing. AI-assisted
checks do not substitute for author verification. This working draft has not
completed final human verification of every proof, source, experiment, and
claim; the statement will be updated to describe the verification actually
completed before submission. Responsibility for the submitted work rests with
the human authors.
