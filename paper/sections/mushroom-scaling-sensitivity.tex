\section{Sensitivity to damping and variance scaling}
\label{sec:mushroom-sensitivity}

The offset $0.01$ in \eqref{eq:conference-damped} is a prespecified heuristic
inherited from the controlled pilot. A separate follow-up declares five offsets
$\tau \in \{0.001,0.003,0.01,0.03,0.1\}$ for $s_j = \sqrt{v_j + \tau}$,
alongside none, SD ($\tau = 0$), and variance scaling ($s_j = v_j$). It reuses
both sample sizes and all 32 partitions per size, restricting the comparison to
centered predictors with a fitted intercept. Each fixed rule receives the same
17 rate factors, two batches, and stopping searches from
Appendix~\ref{sec:mushroom-rate-search}. The five positive offsets together cost
five times the work of one fixed rule; no offset is selected by test loss. The
earlier protocols and artifacts remain unchanged.

Every tested positive offset reduces rare loss relative to none by more than two
paired MCSE. Both risk improvements satisfy that criterion at the tested offsets
$.003$, $.01$, $.03$, and $.1$, including with extra stopping checkpoints. At
$.001$, the aggregate difference is $-.000059$ ($.000069$). The predeclared
criterion requiring both improvements at every positive offset is therefore not
met. Offset $.03$ improves aggregate loss over $.01$ by $.000083$ ($.000019$),
while their rare-loss difference is unresolved. These results do not establish
an optimal offset or cover untested values.

At $n = 256$, all five positive offsets retain both improvements over none at
$M = 8192$ under aggregate validation. Rare-objective selection has a severe
failure at $\tau = .03$: aggregate mean loss is $42.6229$, median $.004108$, and
maximum $1356.8142$; rare mean loss is $4.7479$ and maximum $150.2734$. The
dominant split has two green training examples and one green validation example.
It selects a finite path at $B = 16$, $M = 2048$, and rate factor $1.4$, with
rare validation loss $.000893$. Independent replay passes the declared numerical
gate. The failure remains in all summaries.

Variance scaling has larger losses on the stated grid, but two primary
large-sample selections reach rate factor $6.4$. Across caps and objectives it
has 31 upper-edge decisions, counting repeated splits. Other rules have no rate
boundary selections in this arm. Its tuned ordering therefore remains limited by
the search boundary. The archive retains 139,264 endpoints, 6,144 selections,
1,501 failed paths, and 2,403 failed endpoints. Full paired contrasts, selected
paths, and absence strata are recorded in
\texttt{notes/mushroom-scaling-sensitivity-results.md}.
