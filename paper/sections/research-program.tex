\section{Research program and evaluation}

The first milestone is a finite-time theory for one rare binary feature and
a fixed block of common predictors, supported by a controlled experiment
and a comparison with the closest prior theorems. Write
\[
 C_n=\begin{pmatrix}A_n&c_n\\c_n^\top&b_n\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A_n\to A\succ0,\quad b_n\downarrow0,\quad c_n/b_n\to\kappa.
\]
The target is an account of mean error and covariance along the learning
trajectory that separates incomplete learning of common features, rarity,
and stochastic update moments. Population gradient flow should be extended
first, followed by discrete GD and fresh-sample SGD with explicit scales,
learning rates, batch sizes, and stopping rules. Fixed-data sampling with
replacement and random reshuffling require their own assumptions. None of
these general results is established by the two elementary propositions.

For empirical samples, specify $n\to\infty$, rare prevalence $q_n\downarrow0$,
and whether $nq_n\to\infty$ or rare counts remain bounded. Compare error
contributions with statistical uncertainty, typically of order
$(nq_n)^{-1/2}$ under suitable regularity conditions. Analyze the effects of
estimating centering and scales, and distinguish a stopping time fixed in
advance from one chosen by validation. Full-rank convergence to OLS,
early-stopping regularization, and selection among interpolating solutions
are separate questions.

An initial experiment should use feasible independent, nested, and negatively
associated binary designs, with common-only, rare-only, equal-sign, and
opposite-sign signals. Compare no scaling, standard-deviation scaling,
variance scaling, and damped scaling with separately tuned GD and SGD
learning rates. Vary $Bq$ across its small, intermediate, and large regimes.
Add AdaGrad and Adam as empirical comparators, explicitly recording momentum,
stabilizing constants, clipping, schedules, and weight decay. A fixed diagonal
preconditioner is not a model of their complete dynamics.

Report performance against processed observations as well as update count;
changing batch size changes the cost of an update. Measure original-scale
coefficient error, aggregate excess prediction risk, and excess risk
conditional on rare-feature presence. An isolated rare-coefficient error of
constant size contributes only $O(q_n)$ to population risk. To assess a
consequence beyond aggregate averages, also consider a specified test
prevalence shift that preserves the conditional response model and uses the
training centering and scales at prediction time. New intercept or centering
errors must not be confused with coefficient effects.

The repository's optimization checks compare matrix exponentials with an
independent ODE solution and stochastic moment formulas with exact binomial
batch enumeration. Existing coordinate-descent and Gaussian integration
checks validate the explicit-penalty baselines. These are mathematical
checks, not a completed comparative experiment. A later empirical study should
use sparse text or categorical data, starting with linear models and then a
small neural model. The novelty assessment must identify a conclusion beyond
known early-stopping, adaptive-gradient, and preconditioning theory before
the paper makes a practical recommendation.
