\section{Research program and scope}
The two-feature results isolate a candidate contribution: normalization can
control a feature's own shrinkage while leaving an effect transmitted from
shrinkage of other coefficients. A next step is to establish this mechanism
for a rare binary feature alongside a fixed number of common predictors.
Partition the Gram matrix as
\[
 C_n=\begin{pmatrix}A_n&c_n\\c_n^\top&b_n\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A_n\longrightarrow A\succ0,\quad b_n\downarrow0,\quad c_n/b_n\longrightarrow\kappa.
\]
The research objective is a theorem with explicit sampling, penalty, and
moment assumptions. For scaling exponent $\delta>1/2$ in a formal experiment
with fixed score noise, a candidate rare-coefficient mean is
$\beta_{\mathrm{rare}}^*+\kappa^\top(\beta_{\mathrm{common}}^*
-\E\hat\beta_{\mathrm{common}})$.
This expression is a guide to investigation, not an established sampling
limit: thresholding can persist when coefficient noise vanishes, and
convergence of expectations needs more than convergence in probability.

The first sampling regime should use actual binary triangular arrays with
$nq_{\mathrm{rare},n}\to\infty$, fixed error variance, and stated rates for
both penalties. Regimes with bounded rare counts, multiple rare features,
or data-dependent tuning should be treated separately. Approximation bounds
should then extend Proposition~\ref{prop:bound} to changing supports, rather
than only to an assumed active-sign region.

An empirical study should begin with feasible binary cell probabilities,
varying prevalence, dependence, signal signs, noise, and normalization.
Fixed cell counts isolate the conditional theory. Primary comparisons should
report original-scale bias, variance, selection, and prediction risk at fixed
penalties; tuning rules can then be evaluated as an additional design factor.
The exact selection integral and the nine-region solver provide numerical
checks before larger simulations. The repository includes reproducible
verification code, but this draft makes no claim of a completed empirical
study.

Finally, a targeted literature review must compare the binary covariance
bounds, diagonal-weight limitations, and rare-feature limits with existing
weighted-lasso, correlated-design, and rare-covariate results. The standalone
paper should be organized around whichever results remain both novel and
useful after that review.
